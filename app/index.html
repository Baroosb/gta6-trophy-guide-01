      How this guide was made. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list for Grand Theft Auto VI (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze), as published and as cross-checked against PSNProfiles and Exophase. Every guide is written for this tracker in its own words, and each one names the published sources its facts were checked against — PowerPyx's roadmap, GTABase, GTA Spotter, Six Shore, GTA-Zone and Leonida Interactive among them. Kind, missable flags and the roadmap order are this guide's own layer, reasoned from the official descriptions and those sources. There are no online trophies in GTA VI: the Online filter is here so you can see that for yourself.

      Offline by design. This page loads nothing from the network and works with the network off. Your checklist progress is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back at any time. Links in a guide's sources open only when you choose to follow them.
